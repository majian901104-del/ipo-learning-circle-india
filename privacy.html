How information may be handled when using this informational website.

Information we collect

If you request access to the IPO Desk, we may collect the information you voluntarily submit, including your name, age range, WhatsApp number, email address, city/state, investor type, IPO interests and campaign/source information.

How we use it

We use this information to provide requested community access, send IPO research and market updates, understand which website or advertising source generated an enquiry, and manage follow-up with interested users.

Storage and service providers

Lead information is submitted through Google Apps Script and stored in a Google Sheet controlled by the site operator. The WhatsApp community and other linked services operate under their own privacy terms. A conversion-measurement pixel may load on the community handoff page for advertising attribution.

Your choices

You may choose not to submit the form. If you have joined the community and want your lead record removed or communications stopped, contact the community administrator through WhatsApp. Do not send bank credentials, trading passwords, OTPs or authentication codes through this website or the community.
