Latest announced terms

Reliance Industries disclosed an IPO price band of ₹1,065–₹1,119 per share on 14 October 2026. Public-market reporting places the retail bidding period at 21–23 October, with anchor bidding scheduled for 19 October and expected listing on 28 October. These are dated terms; official issuer and exchange updates take precedence.

Issue size and lot

The reported fresh issue is up to 270 million shares, representing up to approximately ₹30,213 crore at the top of the band. A retail lot is 12 shares, or ₹13,428 at ₹1,119. This is offer information, not a recommendation or an allotment promise.

Research discipline

We separate regulatory confirmation from reported transaction timing. Investors should use the final RHP, exchange notices and issuer announcements for definitive issue terms, price band, dates and risk factors.

Source hierarchy
SEBI DRHP / Reliance disclosure → final offer documents and exchange notices → reputable financial reporting for indicative timing.
