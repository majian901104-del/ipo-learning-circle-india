Offer summary

Moneyview’s public offer opened on 24 September and closed on 28 September 2026. The issue size was ₹1,091.68 crore with a ₹32–34 price band and was subscribed 98.46 times overall.

Listing outcome

The shares opened at ₹55 on NSE on 1 October 2026, representing a 61.76% premium to the ₹34 upper-end issue price. The BSE opening was ₹55.61.

What the market watched

The offer combined a ₹750 crore fresh issue with an offer-for-sale component. Investor attention centred on the company’s digital-first lending model, growth profile and credit quality.

Primary source
Moneyview prospectus filed with SEBI on 29 September 2026. Market and listing data cross-checked against public IPO reporting.
