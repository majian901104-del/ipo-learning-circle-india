IPO NEWS & RESEARCH · 28 November 2026 IST

IPO pipeline: eight issuers advance and two proposals are withdrawn

Independent educational commentary based on dated public reporting; primary filings take precedence.

28 NOV 2026EDITORIAL NOTE · MEDIA SOURCES

Regulatory developments

Moneycontrol and CNBC TV18 reported on 27 November that eight companies had received SEBI observations, including Sterlite Electric, Svatantra Microfin, Gaurik Fashions, Emerald Jewel Industry India, Beauty Garage, Noble Hygiene, MP Steel (India) and Kataria Dhulchand Pannalal Jewellers.

CNBC TV18 also reported withdrawals by Bvishal Oil and Energy and Pentacle Consultants. These changes illustrate that a draft filing does not guarantee an eventual listing.

Research priorities

For industrial businesses, review order-book conversion, working capital and debt. For microfinance, assess collection performance, credit costs and funding. For consumer and jewellery issuers, compare inventory turnover, margins and distribution concentration. These are due-diligence questions, not issuer-specific findings.

What remains unknown

Observation status does not establish the final issue price, bid dates or investment merit. Wait for the final offer document and exchange notice before making comparisons.
