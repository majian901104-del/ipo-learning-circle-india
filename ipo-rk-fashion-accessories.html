Offer structure

The issue is a fresh offer of approximately 42.67 lakh shares. It opened on 5 October and closed on 7 October 2026, with allotment scheduled for 8 October and listing scheduled for 12 October.

Use of proceeds

Reported objects include working capital, a new plating facility at Baruipur, a B2B showroom in Kolkata, completion of B2C stores and inventory for new outlets.

Research focus

Investors should assess the company’s operating scale, working-capital needs, execution of planned retail expansion, customer and supplier concentration, and the risk factors set out in the offer document.
