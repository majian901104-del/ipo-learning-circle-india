Investor Protection · Regulatory Update

Why we no longer publish current IPO GMP figures.

SEBI has cautioned investors about unregulated pre-listing premiums commonly called Grey Market Premium (GMP). Angel Research has discontinued publishing, displaying or circulating current IPO GMP figures and redirects readers to issuer disclosures and recognised exchange information.

SEBI PRESS RELEASEPR 75/202623 November 2026 · Official investor advisory
What changed

GMP is not an exchange price or verified forecast.

SEBI says these figures are unofficial, unverified and unregulated; the transactions occur outside the recognised exchange framework and do not carry exchange settlement guarantees or investor-protection mechanisms.

01 · UNOFFICIAL

No formal price discovery.

Grey-market quotes are not official issue prices, exchange prices or issuer-confirmed information.

02 · INVESTOR PROTECTION

No exchange dispute safeguards.

Transactions outside the recognised exchange framework are not covered by the usual exchange settlement and investor grievance processes.

03 · OUR POLICY

Current GMP publication discontinued.

We prioritise issuer offer documents, SEBI filings, NSE/BSE notices, subscription information from recognised exchanges and actual listing outcomes.
