Methodology & Sources

Every number should tell you what kind of source it is.

Angel Research separates primary facts, reported information and unofficial market indicators so readers can judge evidence quality before interpreting the number.

OFFICIAL / PRIMARYIssuer offer documents, NSE, BSE, SEBI and formal issuer announcements. Used for final issue terms, subscription data and listing outcomes where available.
REPORTEDReputable financial media used for transaction plans or context that has not yet been confirmed in final offer documents.
UNOFFICIALGMP and public grey-market trackers. Timestamped and never presented as exchange data or a return forecast.
INTERNAL ANALYSISAngel Research scoring, comparisons and commentary. These are analytical frameworks, not guaranteed-return predictions.
Correction principle

If a pre-listing expectation differs from the actual listing, both records are preserved. The outcome does not overwrite the earlier snapshot.
