Weekly Primary Markets Brief · Week of 19 October 2026

Jio and Paras lead the 19–23 October IPO outlook.

Two mainboard issues are scheduled for 21–23 October, while several existing offers have 19 October closing deadlines.

Week at a glance

SEBI indexed the red herring prospectuses for Jio Platforms and Paras Healthcare on 15 October. Public bidding is scheduled for 21–23 October, while several other offers have reported 19 October deadlines. Market schedules may change; source documents and exchange notices take priority.

Mainboard calendar

Jio Platforms has a reported price band of ₹1,065–₹1,119 and a 12-share retail lot. Paras Healthcare has a reported ₹660–₹693 band and 21-share lot. Both are scheduled for 21–23 October; Jio anchor bids are due 19 October. The SEBI RHP directory confirms filings, not independent verification of every issue term.

Closing windows and SME proposals

Zetwerk Manufacturing Businesses, Bonfiglioli Transmissions, Tonbo Imaging India and Sahajanand Medical Technologies have scheduled 19 October closing dates. Separately, SEBI's 14 October SME IPO eligibility proposal remains under consultation, not an enacted rule.
