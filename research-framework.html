RESEARCH STANDARDS · TRANSPARENT INVESTMENT EDUCATION

Institutional IPO Research Framework

A repeatable dossier format for assessing Indian IPO issuers. This page is the publication standard, not a completed report on a particular stock, and does not represent a recommendation, privileged allocation or promised return.

Research integrity

Every conclusion needs an evidence trail.

Our reports must separate confirmed issuer filings, market reporting, editorial analysis and unknown facts. A missing offer document, audited statement or pricing reference remains not independently verified until sourced. Publication requires a manual review and a dated record of changes.

Standard IPO Research Dossier

Nine required sections

01 / COVER SHEET

Issuer & report metadata

Exact issuer legal name, segment, report author/editor, published and checked timestamps (IST), version and purpose of the report.

02 / BUSINESS

Model and operating exposure

Revenue streams, customers, competitive position, operating geography and management background, each attributed to a filing or clearly labelled independent analysis.

03 / OFFER STRUCTURE

IPO terms

Issue price, new shares vs OFS, lot size, issue dates, proceeds, shareholder dilution, proposed uses of funds and associated issuer/RHP page references.

04 / FINANCIALS

Audited operating performance

Revenue, profit, margins, cash flow, working capital, leverage, accounting periods and exceptions; no unexplained projected figures or unsourced growth percentages.

05 / VALUATION

Offer valuation and comparables

Enterprise/equity value assumptions, EPS period, P/E or relevant sector multiple, peer selection methodology and sensitivity to assumptions, with calculations that can be reconstructed.

06 / RISKS

Downside and uncertainties

Issuer-specific risk factors, litigation, customer concentration, use-of-proceeds uncertainty, lock-ins, liquidity, SME market rules and operational exposure.

07 / EVIDENCE

Source and factual status

Links to primary filings, secondary reporting and review time for each material assertion. Conflicting sources must remain visible until reconciled.

08 / SCENARIOS

Analytical cases, not guarantees

Base/upside/downside assumptions, disconfirming evidence, valuation ranges where supported and conditions that would change the opinion.

09 / GOVERNANCE

Conflicts, changes and sign-off

Author/editor, role, stated conflicts, corrections, historical version, last checked timestamp and any limitations on regulated research/advisory status.

Publication standard

Evidence before opinion.

Required before “Issuer-confirmed”

Verify the exact primary filing for the relevant IPO and section. A generic SEBI or NSE directory link is a navigation aid, not evidence that the reported price band or lot size has been independently confirmed.

Required before a recommendation

Determine whether an appropriately registered person and applicable review procedures permit publishing investment recommendations. This website’s business collaboration alone does not provide brokerage, custody or guaranteed allocation rights.

The present research library contains historical editorial snapshots. Existing files are not automatically upgraded into full nine-section research reports, and missing sections must not be presented as completed.
