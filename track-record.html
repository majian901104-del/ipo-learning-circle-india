Archive Rules

What we will not do.

The record is designed to make hindsight editing harder.

01 · NO BACKFILL

No retroactive GMP.

If a pre-listing snapshot was not preserved, the field remains blank.

02 · NO CHERRY-PICK

Weak signals stay visible.

Cases where GMP overshoots the listing are kept beside cases where it was close.

03 · ACTUAL OUTCOME

Market open closes the loop.

Once listed, the actual opening replaces no earlier snapshot; it is added beside it.

04 · SOURCE LABEL

Different evidence stays separate.

Official facts, reported information, unofficial indicators and internal analysis are not blended into one label.
