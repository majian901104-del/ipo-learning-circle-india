Evidence hierarchy

Official facts take precedence.

SEBI, NSE, BSE and issuer documents are the preferred authorities for final dates, price bands, lot sizes and offer details. Media articles and IPO-calendar aggregators are supporting references and should never be interpreted as issuer-confirmed merely because they appear here.

Important distinction

“Source linked” means a reference is provided. It does not mean the site automatically retrieved, reconciled or independently authenticated the terms. Our existing dataset is a manual editorial snapshot.

How changes are published

Each new set of figures requires an editor to inspect the original evidence, correct inconsistent entries and update the shared review timestamp. Automated checks identify staleness and schema errors only: they must not silently change a price or imply a successful live verification.

What is not shown

This page does not assert live subscriptions, real-time GMP, guaranteed IPO allotments, guaranteed returns, or confirmed company fundamentals. Missing issuer documents remain a research gap, not an invitation to guess.
