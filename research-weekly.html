Weekly Primary Markets Brief · 05 October 2026

A quieter issue week, but a crowded listing calendar.

One new SME IPO is scheduled to open while 29 companies are expected to make their market debuts during the week.

Week at a glance

After a busy August and September, the new-issue calendar turns quieter. R K Fashion Accessories is the only IPO scheduled to open during the week beginning 5 October, while the listing calendar remains active with 29 expected debuts.

Mainboard calendar

Eight mainboard companies are scheduled to list during the week, including Orient Cables (India), Runwal Enterprises, AceVector, German Green Steel & Power, Shah Investor's Home, SRIT India, Vishal Nirmiti and Nityas Gems & Jewellery.

SME calendar

Twenty-one SME companies are scheduled across the week. The full calendar can be filtered by market segment in the Primary Markets section.
