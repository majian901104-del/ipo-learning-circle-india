Transaction status

Reliance Industries disclosed that Jio Platforms received SEBI’s observation letter on its draft red herring prospectus. Recent reporting indicates the offer may open on 21 October, close on 23 October and target a 28 October listing, subject to market conditions and final transaction documents.

Recent reporting estimates the issue at approximately US$3.8 billion and describes the proposed offer as a fresh-share issue. These figures remain reported terms until final offer documentation confirms them.
