Current IPO calendar

As reviewed on 25 November 2026, Shree Siddhivinayak Greentech Industries is scheduled for 25–27 November at ₹130–₹142. Foodlink F&B Holdings (India) is scheduled for 26–30 November at ₹181–₹191. Dates and reported terms are editorial snapshots; final issuer and exchange notices prevail.

Closed November issues

Crystal Crop Protection, Mahanadi Coalfields and Watrana Rentals have passed their reported bid windows. Allotment and listing outcomes must be confirmed through registrar and exchange disclosures, not inferred from scheduled dates.

SEBI advisory on GMP

On 23 November 2026, SEBI issued PR 75/2026 cautioning investors about unregulated pre-listing premiums commonly called grey-market premium (GMP). SEBI advised registered intermediaries and market participants to refrain from publishing, displaying or circulating these figures. Angel Research has therefore stopped publishing current GMP figures and prioritises issuer documents, exchange notices and verified listing outcomes.
