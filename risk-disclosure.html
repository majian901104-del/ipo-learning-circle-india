Risk Disclosure

IPO signals are not guarantees.

Public-market and IPO investing can result in loss of capital. Subscription demand, GMP, internal scores and past listing outcomes do not assure future performance.

Market and issuer risk

Security prices can move materially because of company-specific, sector, macroeconomic, liquidity, geopolitical and broader market factors.

IPO-specific risk

Newly listed securities may have limited trading history, concentrated ownership, high volatility or weak liquidity. Strong subscription can still be followed by poor market performance.

Unofficial indicators

Grey-market premium is not exchange data. It can vary by source, change rapidly and fail to predict the actual listing price.

Independent verification

Review the final RHP or prospectus, financial statements, risk factors and official exchange or SEBI records before making a decision.
