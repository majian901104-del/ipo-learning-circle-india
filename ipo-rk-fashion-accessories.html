Offer structure

The issue is a fresh offer of approximately 42.67 lakh shares. It opened on 5 October and is scheduled to close on 7 October 2026.

Use of proceeds

Reported objects include working capital, a new plating facility at Baruipur, a B2B showroom in Kolkata, completion of B2C stores and inventory for new outlets.
