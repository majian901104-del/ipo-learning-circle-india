Archive Rules

What we will not do.

The record is designed to make hindsight editing harder.

01 · NO BACKFILL

No retroactive estimates.

Missing historical market observations are not reconstructed or added after the event.

02 · NO CHERRY-PICK

Outcomes stay visible.

Final listing outcomes are preserved beside their issue prices, including negative or weak outcomes.

03 · ACTUAL OUTCOME

Market open closes the loop.

Once listed, the actual opening replaces no earlier snapshot; it is added beside it.

04 · SOURCE LABEL

Different evidence stays separate.

Official facts, reported information and internal analysis are not blended into one label. Current GMP figures are not published.
