Research Principles

Evidence before promotion.

Trust should come from transparent process, not borrowed logos, invented credentials or guaranteed-return language.

01

Separate the sources

Exchange and issuer facts are kept distinct from media reports and unofficial GMP readings.

02

Preserve the timestamp

Market-sensitive observations are attached to a date or snapshot so later outcomes can be compared fairly.

03

Archive the outcome

Listing results are recorded against pre-listing expectations rather than rewriting the story after the event.
