History & Milestones

Three decades across India’s financial markets.

From a capital-markets foundation to an integrated wealth, asset-management and capital-markets platform.

1993

Foundation

The company was incorporated in Mumbai on 20 August 1993, formerly operating as Edelweiss Securities Limited.

2000–05

Investment Banking

The business developed its investment-banking franchise and capital-markets capabilities.

2005–10

Institutional Equities

Institutional equities expanded the platform’s research, sales and trading capabilities.

2020–21

Independent platform

PAG’s majority investment strengthened the platform’s institutional ownership and strategic independence.

2023

Nuvama listing

The business rebranded as Nuvama and listed on NSE and BSE on 26 September 2023.

2026

Integrated financial-services platform

Wealth Management, Asset Management and Capital Markets operate as core businesses serving affluent, UHNI, family-office, corporate and institutional clients.
