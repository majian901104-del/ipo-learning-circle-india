ISSUER RESEARCH NOTE · 28 November 2026 IST

Swaraj Green Power and Fuel: proposed financing and debt review

Independent educational commentary based on dated public reporting; primary filings take precedence.

28 NOV 2026EDITORIAL NOTE · MEDIA SOURCES

Reported offer structure

Moneycontrol reported on 27 November that Swaraj Green Power and Fuel filed an updated draft prospectus for a proposed ₹1,100 crore IPO: ₹800 crore in new shares and ₹300 crore in an offer for sale. The report describes debt reduction as an intended use of proceeds.

Research implications

Fresh equity and secondary sales should be evaluated separately. Debt repayment may lower financing expense but the net effect depends on the actual repayment amount, debt rates, issue expenses and subsequent operating performance. Secondary-sale proceeds do not directly fund the issuer.

Due diligence checklist

Inspect audited cash flows, capacity utilisation, feedstock price sensitivity, customer concentration, working capital, environmental compliance and debt maturity. Reconcile the proposed deployment of capital with the final prospectus.

Unverified information

Final offer dates, price band, valuation and audited financial ratios have not been independently verified for this note. No recommendation or price target is offered.
