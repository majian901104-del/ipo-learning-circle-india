Corporate background

A wealth and financial services platform.

Nuvama’s business spans wealth management, asset management and capital markets. Its corporate identity and registrations can be checked through its official investor information page.

The business collaboration authorization identifies Deepak Agrawal as the independent operator of angelresearchindia.com and covers investor education, factual market communications, digital campaigns and independent promotional publishing. His reported individual Research Analyst registration is INH000011972, separate from the registrations of Nuvama entities. The collaboration does not make Angel Research a Nuvama branch or subsidiary, and does not authorize accepting investor money, securities, passwords, trading orders or KYC on Nuvama’s behalf, or promising preferential IPO allotment.
