Privacy

Privacy notice.

How information may be handled when using this informational website.

Effective date: 07 October 2026. Last updated: 15 October 2026.

Information we collect

If you request access to the IPO Desk, we may collect the information you voluntarily submit, including your name, age range, WhatsApp number, email address, city/state, investor type, IPO interests and campaign/source information.

How we use it

For WhatsApp community registration, we use the required name and phone number to process the requested invitation, keep a registration record and identify the relevant website or advertising source. We send separate research or promotional WhatsApp updates only when the optional, unticked updates-consent checkbox has been selected. Research-update request forms have their own separate purpose and information fields.

Storage and service providers

Existing optional research-update requests are submitted through Google Apps Script and stored in a Google Sheet controlled by the site operator. When the separate WhatsApp community-registration form is enabled, its name and WhatsApp number, campaign/source and optional updates-consent flag are submitted over HTTPS to the Angel Research Cloudflare Worker and stored in an access-controlled Cloudflare D1 database. Those community registration records are scheduled for deletion after 90 days unless a shorter deletion is requested. The group invitation is presented only after successful registration, and contact fields are not placed in the public URL. The explicit optional updates checkbox is not selected by default. The form is not an investment account application. WhatsApp and other linked services operate under their own privacy terms. Optional Google Analytics 4 will be loaded only if a Google Analytics measurement ID is configured and you accept optional analytics. When enabled, Google Analytics may use first-party cookies to measure page visits, general location, traffic sources and clicks on the community invitation. We do not intentionally send contact-form names, WhatsApp numbers or email addresses to Google Analytics. The optional analytics preference is stored in your browser and can be changed through the control on this page. If the independent aggregate conversion service is enabled, it counts broad campaign, page and community-link requests without cookies, persistent identifiers, names, contact details, full URLs, or user-agent storage. Such anonymous operational counts can occur before you make a GA4 choice; explicitly selecting No thanks or using a browser privacy-control signal stops our client-side aggregate collection. The independent service does not prove a successful WhatsApp membership. It may summarize the visitor's country, broad province/state code, device category (mobile, tablet or desktop), and operating-system family without saving a raw browser user-agent string. When a visitor has expressly allowed optional analytics, a compatible browser may additionally provide a device model; any such model is stored only in aggregated event records and displayed only after a minimum number of matching visits. The site uses non-identifying sessionStorage flags in the current browser tab to estimate first page-visit and community-redirect sessions; these estimates are not unique human counts and no random visitor ID is transmitted. Historic events cannot be retroactively deduplicated or identified. Campaign/source fields submitted through the profile form are stored for attribution.

Your choices

Submitting a research-update request is optional. No investment account is opened and no investment service is contracted by submitting this form. You may choose not to submit the form. To request access to or deletion of your submitted registration details, or to withdraw separate WhatsApp research-update consent, email Angel Research at support@angelresearchindia.com. You may contact us without joining the WhatsApp group. For faster handling, please include the mobile number you registered with, but never send passwords, OTPs, trading credentials or bank details. An authorised administrator can delete the corresponding registration in the protected management console. A registration does not guarantee WhatsApp group admission. An optional community invitation is available on the IPO community page. Do not send bank credentials, trading passwords, OTPs or authentication codes through this website or the community.

Privacy contact

Angel Research privacy enquiries and community-registration data requests: support@angelresearchindia.com. This email is an Angel Research contact address and is not represented here as an official Nuvama Wealth Management Limited grievance channel.

External services

Links to exchanges, regulators, issuers, research sources or messaging services are provided for reference. Their content, availability and privacy practices are controlled independently.
