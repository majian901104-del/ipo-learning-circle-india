Methodology & Sources

Trust starts with knowing what kind of evidence you are looking at.

Source Hierarchy

Four evidence classes.

The label tells you how much authority a number carries and whether it can change before final documents are published.

01 · OFFICIAL / PRIMARY

Final facts first.

Issuer offer documents, exchange notices, SEBI records and formal issuer announcements. These take precedence when final terms are available.

02 · REPORTED

Useful, not final.

Reputable financial media may be used for planned dates, transaction size or context before formal documents confirm the terms.

03 · UNOFFICIAL

Sentiment, not exchange data.

GMP and public grey-market trackers are source- and time-dependent. Divergent readings are shown as a range rather than forced into one number.

04 · INTERNAL ANALYSIS

Framework, not forecast.

Angel Research scores and commentary are comparative research tools. They are not guaranteed-return predictions or investment recommendations.

Timestamp & Update Policy

A current-looking number can still be stale.

Time-sensitive fields are presented as snapshots rather than implied continuous feeds.

FINAL

Closed or completed event.

Used for confirmed subscription snapshots or actual listing outcomes. Later corrections remain possible if the authoritative source changes.

SNAPSHOT

Dated market observation.

Used when a dashboard or issue page reflects a specific date or time rather than a continuously streaming data feed.

REPORTED

Source-reported plan.

Used for information such as a proposed launch date before formal offer documents make it definitive.

UNOFFICIAL

Source-dependent market signal.

Used for GMP. A timestamp and source context matter because the reading can change rapidly and trackers can disagree.

Research Evaluation

Read the inputs before forming a view.

Numerical ratings are currently withheld. Public scoring requires defined inputs, weights and a dated record that readers can reproduce.

Inputs we examine

  • Demand strength and category mix.
  • GMP momentum and source consistency, where available.
  • Business and financial quality from public information.
  • Issuer, execution, valuation and market risks.

What the score does not mean

  • It is not a SEBI-registered recommendation.
  • It is not a probability of profit.
  • It is not a guaranteed listing-return forecast.
  • It should not replace the RHP, financial statements or risk-factor review.

Demand, issuer financials, valuation and risks should be reviewed separately. We do not publish an investment recommendation or probability of profit.
