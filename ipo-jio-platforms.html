Transaction status

Reliance Industries disclosed that Jio Platforms received SEBI’s observation letter on its draft red herring prospectus. Reuters reported that the IPO is planned to launch on 21 October, with the shares expected to list on 28 October. These remain source-reported dates until final transaction documents confirm the timetable.

Reported offer scale

Reuters reported that the planned offering is seeking approximately US$3.8 billion. The final offer documents remain the confirmation point for structure, pricing and definitive terms.

Research discipline

We separate regulatory confirmation from reported transaction timing. Investors should use the final RHP, exchange notices and issuer announcements for definitive issue terms, price band, dates and risk factors.

Source hierarchy
SEBI DRHP / Reliance disclosure → final offer documents and exchange notices → reputable financial reporting for indicative timing.
