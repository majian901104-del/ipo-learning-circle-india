Equities and equity funds

Growth potential comes with price volatility and company, sector and market risk. Look at concentration, valuation, fees and the investment horizon.

Fixed income

Income-oriented investments still carry credit, interest-rate and liquidity risks. Understand the issuer, maturity and conditions of repayment.

Funds and managed portfolios

Evaluate the strategy, mandate, benchmark, fees and service provider. Product eligibility and minimum investment depend on the specific offering.

Alternatives and private markets

Private equity, private credit and other alternatives may involve long lock-ups, complex structures and limited liquidity. Availability and suitability are product-specific.
