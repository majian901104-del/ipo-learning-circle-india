Methodology

Four rules for reading GMP.

The dashboard keeps unofficial indicators in context.

01 · TIMESTAMP

Every GMP needs a time.

A premium without a timestamp can become stale within hours.

02 · SOURCE

Unofficial means source-dependent.

When trackers disagree, the range is more honest than one number.

GMP FAQ

Read the signal correctly.

Quick answers for investors using grey-market indicators.

Is GMP official exchange data?

No. GMP is an unofficial, unregulated grey-market indication and can vary by source and time.

Does a high GMP guarantee listing gains?

No. SRIT India is a current example: pre-listing GMP implied a much stronger debut than the actual NSE opening.

Why show multiple GMP readings?

When public trackers materially disagree, showing the range makes the uncertainty visible.

What should be read alongside GMP?

Offer terms, subscription, issuer fundamentals, valuation context, risk factors and official documents.
